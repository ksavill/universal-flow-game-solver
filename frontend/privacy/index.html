This notice covers the browser edition at flowpuzzlesolver.net.

Updated September 30, 2026

Puzzle images and processing

Images you select, photograph or paste are processed in your browser. The site downloads the software needed to detect and solve boards; it does not upload those images or puzzle contents to a solving server.

Saved puzzles and reports

The local library uses browser storage on this device. Saved puzzles and locally saved problem reports remain there until you remove them or clear site data. Reports are limited to 50 entries and 5 MB; reports older than 30 days are removed when the local inbox is opened. You can preview a report and choose whether to include a resized image. Reports are not sent automatically.

Exported puzzle and report files are saved to the location you choose. If you later share a file or post an issue, its contents go to the recipient or service you select. Do not include personal or sensitive information in public issue reports.

Hosting

Cloudflare delivers this site and its software downloads. As with other websites, serving a request involves network information such as your IP address, browser information, requested address and time of access. Puzzle images selected in the solver are not part of these requests. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for its handling of service data.

Advertising

The puzzle guide offers optional Google AdSense advertising. Google is contacted only after you select Show optional ad on the guide. This choice is not remembered on a new page visit. Turn off ads reloads the guide without advertising; Privacy choices lets you revisit the choices provided by Google's consent message when available. Site approval and ad availability determine whether an ad can be shown. The solver and this privacy page do not load Google advertising.

The solver does not require viewing or clicking an advertisement. Guide-page advertising is labeled and kept separate from puzzle processing. We do not send your puzzle images, board contents or local reports to advertisers.

If you enable optional Google ads, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and receive information about your browser, device and page visit to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads. Google's consent messages provide applicable regional consent and opt-out choices. Declining consent does not prevent reading or solving.

You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage Google advertising preferences in My Ad Center.

Your choices

You can use the solver without creating an account. Remove saved puzzles through the library, or clear this site's data in your browser to remove local storage. Export puzzles first if you want to keep them. Blocking third-party advertising does not prevent puzzle solving.

Questions

For questions about the site, use the project's issue tracker. Issues there are public; keep your message free of private information.
